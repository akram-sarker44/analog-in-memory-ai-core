## 📌 Project Overview
A proof-of-concept discrete hardware prototype executing real-time artificial intelligence inference at the speed of light using fundamental physics (Ohm's Law & Kirchhoff's Current Law) without traditional digital Von Neumann CPUs or GPUs.

## 🔬 Experimental Hardware Results
- **Operational Amplifier:** Texas Instruments LM358 Single-Supply Dual Op-Amp (DIP-8)
- **Supply Voltage ($V_{CC}$):** 9.72 V DC
- **High-State Decision Output (Active/Class 1):** `9.55 V`
- **Low-State Grounded Output (Quiescent/Class 0):** `0.00 V`
- **Execution Speed:** Instantaneous (Sub-microsecond physical analog propagation)

## 🦾 Target Application: Biomedical EMG Prosthetics
Designed to directly interface with differential surface Electromyography (EMG) sensors (e.g., Varendra University HMI Armband) to trigger robotic prosthetic actuation with near-zero energy consumption.
